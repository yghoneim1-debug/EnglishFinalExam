Grammar Master Rules Cheat Sheet

1. Gerund = -ing form used as a noun
Example: Swimming improves stamina. (Test: Replace with "the activity".)
2. Four Common Gerund Jobs
Subject (Reading helps), Direct Object (We enjoy reading), Predicate Nominative (Her hobby is reading), Object of Preposition (Good at reading).
3. Gerund Phrases
Includes the gerund plus its modifiers/complements. Example: Reading mystery novels at night relaxes me.
4. Participle = verb form used as an adjective
Present (-ing) or Past (-ed/-en/irregular). Example: The barking dog; The broken glass.
5. Participial Phrases
Modifies a noun/pronoun. Ends before the main clause. Example: Startled by thunder, the cat hid.
6. Gerund vs. Participle
Singing makes her happy (Gerund = noun job). The singing child smiled (Participle = describes child).
7. Concrete vs. Abstract Nouns
Concrete = sensed by 5 senses (apple, thunder, fragrance, snow). Abstract = qualities, feelings, ideas (kindness, loyalty, jealousy, freedom).
8. Watch for Progressive/Past Verb Phrases
"The chef was chopping onions" = progressive verb phrase (NOT a gerund or participle).
